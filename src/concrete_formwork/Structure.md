## Structure

* Use different materials for each structural component, including:

  * Concrete
  * Formwork
  * Bracing
  * Soil
  * Other relevant elements

* Generate different geometry types depending on the component:

  * **Formwork:** line geometry.
  * **Concrete:** surface geometry.
  * **Bracing:** line geometry.
  * **Soil:** surface geometry.
  * **Reference/cutting surface:** represent it as a line or horizon used to cut the formwork. Depending on its position relative to the formwork, the resulting region should be either filled or empty.

* Wait until the **plot files are up to date** before reworking the geometry.
